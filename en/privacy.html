Privacy Policy

1. Data controller

Montilabs
Contact email: hola@montilabs.cl

2. Data we collect

Montilabs collects the following data:

  • Contact details: your name, company (optional), email address and message, when you write to us through the contact form or by email.
  • Browsing and advertising data: only if you accept cookies. Google may record, for example, the pages you visit, clicks on our ads and contact links, your device and your IP address.

If you reject cookies, we do not collect browsing data. We do not store your data in a database of our own: the contact form is processed by Formspree, which forwards your message to us by email (Formspree's privacy policy).

3. Purpose of processing

  • To answer questions and requests for information sent through the contact form or by email.
  • To improve the user experience on the website.
  • To measure visits and the performance of our ads, if you accept cookies.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on the consent of the data subject, given when you voluntarily contact us or accept cookies, in accordance with Chile's Law 19.628 on the Protection of Private Life.

5. Cookies and local storage

This site uses Google Tag Manager and Google Ads to measure visits and our ads. These services may set Google cookies and are only activated if you accept them in the cookie notice. If you reject them, they are not loaded.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. You can change it at any time: open cookie preferences.

More information in Google's privacy policy.

6. International transfers

Messages sent through the contact form are processed and stored on Formspree servers located in the United States.

If you accept cookies, Google may process browsing and advertising data on servers located outside Chile, including in the United States. If you reject them, no such transfer takes place.

7. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: find out what data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data.
  • Objection and restriction: object to or restrict the processing.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format.

To exercise these rights, email us at hola@montilabs.cl. You can also exercise your rights before Chile's Council for Transparency (Consejo para la Transparencia).

8. Data retention

Messages we receive, by email or through the contact form, are kept only as long as needed to answer the inquiry, unless a business relationship is established that requires a longer period. Browsing and advertising data are kept according to the settings of Google's services. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
